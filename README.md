## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
We propose a Direct Farm-to-Consumer Platform that connects farmers directly with customers through a mobile application or website. Farmers can upload their products, set prices, and receive orders directly from consumers. This reduces the need for middlemen and makes fresh agricultural products easily available.
2. How It Addresses the Problem
The platform eliminates unnecessary intermediaries, allowing farmers to earn more profit for their products while consumers can purchase fresh vegetables, fruits, and other farm products at affordable prices. It also improves transparency in pricing and supports farmers' income.
3. Innovation and Uniqueness of the Solution
Our solution provides a simple digital marketplace with direct ordering, transparent pricing, online payments, and delivery tracking. Its uniqueness is that it creates a direct connection between farmers and consumers, ensuring fair earnings for farmers and affordable, fresh products for customers.

## Technical Approach1. Technologies to Be Used:
We use HTML, CSS, JavaScript for frontend, Python with Flask for backend, and MySQL for database management. Smartphones, laptops, and internet connectivity are used.
2. Methodology and Process for Implementation:
Farmers register on the platform and upload product details and prices. Consumers browse products, place orders, and make payments directly. Farmers receive orders and arrange delivery, reducing middlemen and ensuring fair prices for both farmers and consumers.

## Feasibility and Viability
1. Feasibility of the Idea
The proposed platform is technically and economically feasible using simple web and mobile technologies. It requires low investment and can benefit both farmers and consumers through direct sales.
2. Potential Challenges and Risks
Challenges include farmers' lack of technical knowledge, delivery difficulties, internet connectivity issues, product spoilage, and payment security.
3. Strategies for Overcoming These Challenges
Provide simple user-friendly features and farmer training, arrange reliable delivery services, use secure online payment methods, and ensure proper product storage and timely delivery.

## Impact and Benefits
1. Potential Impact on the Target Audience:
The platform helps farmers earn more income and allows consumers to buy fresh agricultural products at affordable prices.
2. Benefits of the Solution:
It provides social support to farmers, improves economic benefits by reducing middlemen, and helps the environment by reducing food waste and unnecessary transportation.
